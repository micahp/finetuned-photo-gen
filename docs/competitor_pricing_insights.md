### Key Take-aways

1. **Runway sets the benchmark** – $30 buys 40-90 short clips if users choose Gen-4 Turbo (5 cr/s).
2. **Higgsfield** is less efficient but still delivers ~20 clips at $39.
3. **Krea’s compute model looks generous on paper** (hundreds of clips) but CU equivalence is debated by users.
4. **Your current pricing allows only 2-5 clips** in the Creator tier, creating adoption friction.
5. **Reducing video credit cost to 8-25 cr/s raises capacity to 4-12 clips** – competitive if you emphasise quality, watermark-free content, or bundled image credits.

### Sensitivity Checks

| Scenario | Weighted Cost / Credit | Creator GM % (500 cr) | Notes |
|----------|-----------------------|-----------------------|-------|
| Base (62 % images / 38 % video) | $0.0044 | **90 %** | Healthy margin |
| Video share ↑ to 60 % | $0.0064 | **82 %** | Margin drops 8 pp |
| Supplier price +25 % | $0.0055 | **87 %** | Margin drops 3 pp |
| 200 % credit utilisation (roll-over cap) | $0.0044 | **48 %** | Only if heavy users burn double credits |

### Recommendations

* Keep budget video models at **8–12 cr/s**, premium models up to **25 cr/s** to protect margins.
* Offer **credit packs** for overages instead of unlimited roll-over.
* Track real image/video credit mix to refine pricing quarterly.
